## What is MiBlend?
MiBlend is a powerful Blender addon designed to **simplify and optimize the process of creating 3D content for Minecraft**. It offers flexible settings and minimally modifies materials, ensuring better compatibility and predictability in your workflow.

---

## What are the differences between MiBlend and MCprep?
These are **two completely different addons**, developed by independent teams. 

Although some ideas from MCprep were adapted into MiBlend, their implementation and approach to development are **fundamentally different**. 
MiBlend strives for minimal interference, modern solutions, and better performance.

## Which is better: MCprep or MiBlend?
There's no definitive answer, as both addons are **oriented towards different goals and needs**:

* **MiBlend** utilizes **modern technologies**, supports the latest Blender and Minecraft versions, and provides **more flexible and advanced settings**.
* **MCprep** focuses on **backward compatibility** and maximum ease of use.

---

## Why do I need MiBlend?
MiBlend was created to address a number of significant issues present in MCprep:

* **Incorrect operation of many rigs** in newer Blender versions, as they were created a long time ago.
* **Problems with animated textures**.
* **Multiple issues with block and item spawning**.
* **Confusing node structure** in materials, complicating their setup.
* **Strong interference with different parts of Blender**. This makes it impossible to use MCprep in non-standard situations.
* **Slow development and bug fixes**. Serious issues can remain unresolved for years.
* [**Other unresolved issues**](https://github.com/Moo-Ack-Productions/MCprep/issues) on GitHub.

MiBlend was developed as a **more modern, flexible, and convenient tool** that eliminates these shortcomings. Although the addon is currently in beta, it will be fully refined and optimized by the 1.0 release.

---

## Can MiBlend be used with MCprep?
Technically, it's possible, but **highly discouraged**. MiBlend uses its own scene and material handling algorithms, which may **conflict with MCprep** or lead to unpredictable results and errors in your projects.

---

## What happened to Mcblend?
The addon was **renamed to MiBlend** to avoid confusion with another independent project named Mcblend, created by developer **Nosiq**. This helped to differentiate the projects and avoid misunderstandings.
