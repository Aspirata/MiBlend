---
title: 🧪 Installing Experimental Versions
description: A detailed guide on how to install experimental versions of MiBlend.
---

<Warning>
  **Warning!** Experimental versions may contain **critical bugs** and be unstable. Only install them for testing new features, exploring the addon, or at your own risk. Do not use them in important projects!
</Warning>

---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Builds Page" icon="github">
    Click [this link to go to GitHub Actions](https://github.com/Aspirata/MiBlend/actions). Here you'll find all automatic MiBlend builds.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the Desired Commit" icon="list">
    The **list of commits** is sorted by date: the **newest builds are at the top**, and older ones are below. Choose the one you need.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in to GitHub" icon="user-check">
    **Downloading artifacts is only available to authorized GitHub users.** If you haven't logged into your account yet, please do so.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download the Artifact" icon="download">
    Scroll down the page to the **"Artifacts"** section. Find and click on `MiBlend Experimental Auto Build` to download the ZIP archive with the addon.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install the Addon in Blender" icon="file-arrow-down">
    Install the downloaded ZIP archive in Blender **as a regular addon**.

    **Important:** It's recommended to **restart Blender** after installation for changes to take effect correctly.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Done!" icon="check-circle">
    Congratulations! The experimental version of MiBlend has been successfully installed and is ready for use.
  </Step>
</Steps>
